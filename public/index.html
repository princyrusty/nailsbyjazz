Sizing guide

Find your fit

  1. Stick clear tape across the widest part of each nail.
  2. Mark both edges with a pen, peel it off and measure in millimetres.
  3. Match your sizes to the chart. Between two sizes? Pick the larger one and file it down.

Not sure? Choose Custom fit (₹100) and send your 10 measurements. Jazz matches each nail for you.

Questions

Good to know

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

We deliver to your home anywhere in India. Shipping is a flat ₹99 per order, and your nails arrive in 5–6 days.

How long do press-on nails last?

With glue, 1–2 weeks. With adhesive tabs, 1–3 days, which is ideal for a single function. Removed gently, a set can be worn again 3–5 times.

What does the custom fee include?

Custom sizing for each of your 10 nails, plus design changes such as a different base colour, initials, or matching an outfit. It's also what we charge to recreate a design you send us.

How do I pay?

Place your order here and it opens on WhatsApp. Jazz confirms it and shares UPI details (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). Direct online payment on the website is coming soon.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Create an account before you check out, and your order status and tracking number appear in My account.
